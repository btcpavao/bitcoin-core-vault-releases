Bitcoin logo artwork provenance

Identified project assets: tools/core-offline/branding/bitcoin.png,
usr/share/core-vault/bitcoin-source.png, usr/share/core-vault/bitcoin-ui.png,
and the installer branding/core-offline/bitcoin.png.

The maintainer-supplied project bitcoin-logo.webp decodes pixel-for-pixel to
Wikimedia Commons' 960 x 960 rendering of File:Bitcoin.svg. The project 128-pixel
branding is a near-identical Lanczos raster derivative of this image; the precise
historical download URL/resampler was not recorded. The 64-pixel UI image is an
exact Lanczos resize of the project 128-pixel branding. These images are not
asserted to be copied from Bitcoin Core 31.1's different bitmap resources.

Underlying standard logo: bitboy. The creator's current PNG and vector release
posts expressly dedicate his graphics to the public domain. The posts were
originally made in November 2010 and last edited on July 10, 2014; this notice
does not claim that the public-domain wording was already present in 2010.
https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=1631.msg19455
https://bitcointalk.org/index.php?topic=1756.msg21619

The matching standalone SVG reference is documented on Wikimedia Commons as a
derivative of the bitboy logo and currently credits Grayliptrot. Commons marks
it public domain using its PD-textlogo/copyright-originality classification.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bitcoin.svg
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bitcoin_logo.svg

reference.svg and reference-960.png preserve the matching public references;
provenance.json preserves hashes, sources and the measured comparison limits.
The underlying logo's public-domain status is not the project's MIT license.
Attribution here records provenance and does not imply upstream sponsorship.
No production artwork was replaced or changed during this audit.
